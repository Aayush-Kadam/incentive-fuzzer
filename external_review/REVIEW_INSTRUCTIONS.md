# Review Instructions

1. Record expertise, conflicts, review date, and source material actually consulted.
2. Compare the encoded rule component with the cited frozen source. Do not infer fidelity from the project summary alone.
3. Review the threat model separately from rule fidelity: actor, information, feasible action, timing, cost, legality, and omitted responses.
4. Review normalized monetary or coverage valuations explicitly. A sourced threshold does not validate a project-authored valuation.
5. Decide whether the reported finding maps to the claimed phenomenon, to a different phenomenon, to intended response, or to model artifact.
6. Check that the wording remains component-level and domain-scoped.
7. Treat repair outputs as technical counterfactuals, never recommendations, unless a separate normative and empirical review supports them.
8. Return the completed form without changing benchmark labels. Material corrections require a new version and an audit trail.

Recommended dispositions are `ACCEPT`, `ACCEPT_WITH_CHANGES`, `REJECT_ENCODING`, `INSUFFICIENT_EXPERTISE`, and `NEEDS_ADDITIONAL_SOURCE`.
