# Claim Review Guide

Allowed claims are component-level, bounded, reproducible, and explicit about project-authored assumptions. Reviewers should reject wording that converts curated benchmark fractions into population accuracy, formalizes omitted economics by implication, treats modeled opportunities as observed behavior, or presents bounded repairs as recommendations.

The governing snapshot is `research/M8_ENTRY_CLAIM_LEDGER.md`. Independent review remains unresolved until actual external feedback is received and versioned.
