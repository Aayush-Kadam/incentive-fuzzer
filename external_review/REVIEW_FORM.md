## Rule fidelity

- Does the encoded component match the cited frozen source?
- Are comparison strictness, units, dates, and schedule parameters correct?
- What material rule components are omitted?

## Threat model and actions

- Are the declared actions economically plausible?
- Are they legally or administratively feasible? Distinguish legality from mathematical feasibility.
- Which important responses or constraints are missing?
- Are action costs and information assumptions defensible?

## Valuation

- Are monetary/payoff normalizations defensible for the stated narrow claim?
- What alternative valuations could reverse the result?

## Pathology mapping

- Does the finding correspond to the documented phenomenon, a different phenomenon, intended response, or model artifact?
- Is the IF-CWE mapping appropriate?

## Scope and repairs

- Are the conclusions narrow enough?
- Are repair outputs clearly technical counterfactuals rather than policy recommendations?
- Which public wording should be prohibited?
