# License Decision Required Before Publication

No license is automatically selected in this research preview. The project lead must choose the intended software reuse terms before making the repository public. An OSI-approved permissive license such as Apache-2.0 or MIT may be suitable for original code, but this decision depends on author intent.

External rule texts, citations, and encoded facts retain their own attribution and rights. A repository software license must not be represented as relicensing external source material. Until a license is selected, public redistribution and reuse permissions remain unspecified.
