## Reading

The broad claim is not defensible as novelty: all core verbs already exist in adjacent literatures. The potentially defensible unit is a tested protocol and common evidence format spanning economic-rule execution, actor-feasible deviations, economic properties, domain-aware reduction, robustness, and repair regression. Even that is a hypothesis until a systematic review and benchmark comparison show no equivalent system and demonstrate measurable integration value.
