# M7 Hostile Review

## Verdict

No critical leakage or reproducibility defect remains, but external-validity limitations are substantial. PASS WITH LIMITATIONS is the strongest defensible verdict.

## Were cases cherry-picked to resemble synthetic fixtures?

Yes, representability screening favors scalar thresholds. The exclusion register makes this visible, but it means v0.1 tests the current formal fragment rather than a representative universe of institutions.

## Are rediscoveries merely threshold arithmetic?

Mostly. Boundary-only found the same 19 cases as combined search, with fewer total evaluations (369 versus 1,261). The integrated workflow adds label isolation, replay, reduction, and formal evidence—not superior recall on this suite.

## Did labels leak?

Automated field scans and API separation found no leakage. Runtime files contain no expected class or pathology. Labels are loaded only after runs are frozen.

## Are encodings faithful and actions realistic?

Thresholds and phase-out directions match sources, but benefit valuations and actions are author-selected. All external cases are partial. The Arizona case demonstrates that threshold data alone cannot support an additive interaction model.

## Were misses or exclusions hidden?

No. Arizona is a preserved miss. Eighteen pre-run exclusions are tabulated by reason.

## Is the suite broad enough for general claims?

No. Ten external scalar components from US tax, benefits, health eligibility, retirement, and procurement are not a representative sample.

## Does SMT add value?

It independently confirmed six external violations and three scoped non-violations. It did not improve candidate recall because the runner used it as confirmation rather than an independent candidate source.

## Are surprise findings artifacts?

No surprise findings occurred. That avoids unresolved false positives but provides no evidence about novel-discovery handling beyond protocol tests.

## Are repair results useful beyond obvious phase-outs?

Not established. Two are obvious smoothing families; the FAR repair is an enforcement-cost proxy. They test regression plumbing only.

## Are external strategic cases absent?

Yes. No credibly sourced finite-payoff game survived screening.

## Is IF-Bench self-authored?

The rules are externally sourced, but cases, valuations, actions, labels, and matching decisions remain project-authored. Independent economist review is still required.

## Strongest surviving criticism

Success is conditional on project-authored action spaces and normalized valuations, and the headline recall is driven by easy scalar boundaries. The benchmark supports component-level structural rediscovery, not real-world auditing accuracy.
