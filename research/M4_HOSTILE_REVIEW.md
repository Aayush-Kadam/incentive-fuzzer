# M4 Hostile Review

## Strongest objections

1. The population is a weighted list of isolated optimizers, not a population model with interaction.
2. All weights and behavioral parameters are synthetic; numerical precision can disguise assumption uncertainty.
3. B1's large false-negative rate is partly manufactured by candidate ordering.
4. B2's hurdle is arbitrary and makes response suppression tautological for small-gain fixtures.
5. Multipliers have no effect where base action cost is zero; fixed friction carries most scholarship sensitivity.
6. The same team authored engine, fixtures, grids, and holdout, so the holdout is only an implementation check.
7. M3 does not prove population aggregation or the exogenous cost overlay.

## Response

These objections are valid scope constraints, not hidden qualifications. M4 labels every parameter as synthetic or assumed, publishes all cells and individual records, separates opportunity from response, reports B1 error against exhaustive B0, and states the formal boundary explicitly. The milestone demonstrates an auditable robustness workflow; it does not validate a behavioral or empirical theory.

## Residual risk

The principal scientific risk is model misspecification, followed by action-set incompleteness and dependence on hand-authored fixtures. More tests cannot eliminate those risks. External mechanisms, independently sourced calibrations, and genuinely interactive models are needed before policy claims.
