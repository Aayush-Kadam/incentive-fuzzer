# M7 Exclusions

The candidate funnel screened 28 external candidates, excluded 18, and included 10.

| Candidate family | Count | Predeclared reason |
|---|---:|---|
| Housing-choice voucher schedules | 3 | Local payment standards and household deductions not frozen reliably |
| TANF earnings disregards | 3 | State/time rules and benefit calculation not captured by scalar model |
| Corporate bonus/commission plans | 3 | No authoritative public contract with stable payoff details |
| Emissions and renewable-credit programs | 3 | Banking, verification, and multi-period dynamics materially omitted |
| Insurance reimbursement/deductible designs | 2 | Contract-specific coinsurance and network terms unavailable |
| Education aid/Pell formulas | 2 | FAFSA formula complexity exceeds honest v0.1 representation |
| Platform incentives | 2 | Public documentation insufficient for payoff/action fidelity |

No candidate was removed because of an observed benchmark miss. The Arizona interaction was retained despite the miss.
