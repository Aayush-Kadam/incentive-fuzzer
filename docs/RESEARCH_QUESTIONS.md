# Research Questions

## Primary

**RQ1.** Can heterogeneous economic rules share a declarative semantics rich enough for economically meaningful deviations yet restricted enough for deterministic execution and solver translation?

**RQ2.** Does a portfolio of boundary, property-based, optimization, and symbolic search find more valid vulnerabilities than matched random search and expert-written edge cases under equal budgets?

**RQ3.** Can economic counterexample reduction minimize agents, variables, magnitude, periods, and rule components while preserving feasibility, profitability, and the same property violation?

**RQ4.** Do candidate repairs reduce held-out exploitability without unacceptable changes in budget, participation, equity, administrative complexity, or intended response?

## Secondary

- When do evaluator and solver semantics remain equivalent for piecewise-linear rules?
- How sensitive are findings to manipulation costs, information, enforcement, and behavioral models?
- Which vulnerability classes transfer across tax-benefit, procurement, and commercial incentive domains?
- When is equilibrium analysis necessary, and which solution concept matches the institution?
- Can a benchmark avoid rewarding obvious threshold detection while remaining labelable and reproducible?

## Falsifiers

- Most accepted findings disappear under weakly plausible costs or feasibility constraints.
- Search gains vanish against boundary enumeration on nontrivial benchmarks.
- Shrinking changes the economic cause of a violation.
- Repairs overfit known attacks or merely move a discontinuity.
- Cross-domain support requires bespoke semantics rather than adapters to a common core.
